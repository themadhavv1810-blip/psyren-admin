PSYREN Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

PSYREN connects people in Delhi who need local services (customers) with skilled workers, and offers a "Connect" space for business ideas and teams. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, a mobile number you type in (we do not verify it by SMS), your role (customer or worker), and optionally your age.
  • Worker profile: service type, experience, skills, bio, PIN code and locality, portfolio photos and project details you add, skill test results, and an optional map pin of your work area.
  • Location: only when you choose to use GPS or pick a place on the map. For "Find nearest worker", a rounded location (about 100 m) is visible to nearby workers; your exact address, phone number and exact point are visible only to you and the worker who accepts your request. Your own "my location" for distance display is saved only on your phone.
  • Activity in the app: bookings, urgent requests, contracts, reviews, reports, collaboration requests, project chat messages, ideas and payment confirmations you make inside the app.
  • Device token: a push-notification token, so the app can alert you about bookings and requests.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, show workers to customers, and let the two sides contact each other.
  • To send notifications about your bookings, urgent requests, Connect requests and payment updates.
  • To keep the platform safe: reports, blocking, and admin review of complaints.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertisements.

3. Who can see what

  • Worker profiles (name, service, experience, rating, locality, portfolio) are visible to signed-in users.
  • Your phone number is shared with the other person only for a booking, request or accepted connection you take part in.
  • Admins of PSYREN can view account and booking data to handle complaints and verification.

4. Services that process data for us

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) stores accounts and app data and delivers notifications.
  • Cloudflare Workers relays notification requests; it reads only the booking or request involved in an event.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles; loading a map sends your device's IP address and the visible map area to its servers.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

You can edit your profile any time. You can delete your account inside the app (Profile, "Delete my account"). This removes your login, profile, worker profile and photos, your team posts, skill-exchange posts, monthly plan requests, co-founder profile and the phone-number lock. Records that involve other people (for example past bookings, reviews and ideas that someone bought) may remain with your name so the other person's history stays correct.

6. Security

Data is stored with Firebase and protected by access rules so users can read only what the app allows. No system is perfectly secure; keep your password private.

7. Children

PSYREN is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For questions or data requests, write to the developer email shown on the PSYREN app listing, or use the in-app Report option.
